# Business Workflow

## Customer Purchase Journey

1. Customer visits the website.

2. Customer browses available products.

3. Customer views product details.

4. Customer adds products to the shopping cart.

5. Customer reviews the shopping cart.

6. Customer proceeds to checkout.

7. Checkout Service validates the shopping cart.

8. Payment Service processes the payment.

9. Shipping Service calculates shipping costs.

10. Checkout Service confirms the order.

11. Email Service sends the order confirmation.

12. Customer receives confirmation and the order enters fulfillment.
