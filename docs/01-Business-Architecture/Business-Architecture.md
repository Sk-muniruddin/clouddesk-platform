# Business Architecture

## Overview

The CloudDesk Commerce Platform supports the complete online purchasing lifecycle from product discovery to successful order completion.

The platform is designed around business capabilities rather than technical implementation.

---

## Core Business Capabilities

- Product Browsing
- Product Search
- Shopping Cart Management
- Checkout
- Payment Processing
- Shipping Calculation
- Order Confirmation
- Product Recommendation

---

## Business Workflow
