# Business Requirements

## 1. Overview

The CloudDesk Commerce Platform is a cloud-native e-commerce application designed to provide customers with a seamless online shopping experience. The platform enables users to browse products, manage shopping carts, securely complete purchases, and receive order confirmations while providing administrators with the ability to manage products and orders.

---

## 2. Business Objectives

- Provide a reliable online shopping platform.
- Enable secure online payments.
- Improve customer shopping experience.
- Support business growth through scalable architecture.
- Ensure high availability and fault tolerance.
- Reduce operational overhead using managed cloud services.

---

## 4. Target Users

### Customers

- Browse products
- Add items to cart
- Place orders
- Track purchases

### Administrators

- Manage products
- Manage inventory
- Monitor orders
- Manage platform operations

---

## 5. Functional Requirements

The platform shall allow customers to:

- Browse products
- Search products
- View product details
- Add products to cart
- Update cart
- Remove cart items
- Checkout
- Make online payments
- Receive confirmation emails

The platform shall allow administrators to:

- Manage products
- Monitor application health
- View order information

---
